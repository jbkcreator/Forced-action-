What Changed: v7 → v9

v9 preserves every element of v7 intact. Architecture, infrastructure, revenue ladder, build sequence, and all 36 Hari questions are unchanged. v9 selectively merges 4 of v8’s 10 growth layers — the ones that directly accelerate the commercial engine — incorporates an advisory acceleration pass that tightens monetization timing and retention, adds explicit conversion funnel assumptions with rollback criteria, defines concrete Cora autonomy guardrails, introduces multi-ICP expansion channels behind gates, and includes kill-switch discipline for every channel and feature.




v8 Layers Incorporated


Layer 3: Cora Conversational Closer. Claude Sonnet-powered contextual SMS for lock close using live ZIP data, lead counts, and competitor activity. Voice drop via Synthflow for high-intent non-converters. “I Found You a Deal” proactive lead alerts. Annual push at deal-win moment. Primarily prompt engineering, not a build task.


Layer 8: Self-Healing Ops. Automated incident response when first-payment rate drops below 25% for 48 hrs. Stripe failed payment recovery: 3-step SMS (Day 1 soft reminder, Day 3 urgency with missed-lead data, Day 5 downgrade to $97 Data-Only). Vendor cost monitoring via n8n.


Layer 5: Intelligence Flywheel (Learning Card only). Weekly Learning Card: Sunday midnight LangGraph job summarizes message_outcomes, deal_outcomes, and A/B results into learning_cards table. Cora reads at start of every decision tree. Schema Stage 1, Sunday job Stage 7.


Layer 2: Referral Core Loop. 60-second referral notification via Redis. Milestone escalation (1 ref = 5 credits, 3 refs = free month, 5 refs = Lock upgrade). Forward pack with Claude-written copy per trade. Team mechanic and leaderboard deferred to Stage 5+.




v8 Layers Preserved at Original Staging


Layer 1: Paid Acquisition Engine. Meta/Google ads activate Week 3–4 AFTER first-payment rate validates at ≥30%. Not Day 1.


Layer 4: Autonomous County Launch. Gate monitoring + Slack 1-tap at Stage 6. Full 48-hr sequence at Stage 7.


Layer 6: Pricing Automation. Trade + county cohort pricing at Stage 10 (requires 6+ weeks deal data).


Layer 7: Partner Channel. Multi-location outreach at Stage 7. Supplier Intelligence at Stage 12.


Layer 9: Contractor Benchmark Moat. Benchmark reports Stage 12. Public market report Stage 13.




Advisory Acceleration Pass


#A1: First-session monetization wall. Every free user hits a payment moment in Session 1. Countdown on blurred-lead access. ROI frame before payment.


#A2: Abandonment pressure, not nurture. 10–15 min no-payment → automated SMS with single CTA. Click-no-complete → scarcity follow-up. Stop nurturing users who show buy intent.


#A3: Faster wallet push. Saved-card users pre-qualified for wallet. Wallet offer immediately after repeated usage. “Missing leads” framing for pay-per-action users.


#A4: Stronger urgency defaults. Every strong lead creates a short-lived urgency window. Every active ZIP visibly shows competition pressure. Auto lock messaging for repeated ZIP activity. Instant monetization on every weather event.


#A5: Earlier retention triggers. 5–7 day inactivity → auto save flow for lock holders. Wallet disengagement → smaller commitment offer. “What you would have missed” summaries. Deal win → immediate ROI upsell.


#A6: Learning with guardrails. Cora can change messaging, timing, routing, urgency framing, and small pricing tests within strict guardrails. See Cora Guardrail Ranges section. 10% traffic first. Auto-rollback if metrics drop.
