## What Forced Action is

Forced Action is a lead delivery service for service-business owners.
We monitor public distress signals (storms, foreclosures, tax
delinquencies, code violations, probate, bankruptcies, deed transfers,
liens, judgments) and send qualified, real-time leads in the ZIP
codes a subscriber has locked. Subscribers can contact homeowners
first because they get the signal first.

## Who it's for (verticals supported)

- Roofing contractors
- Restoration contractors
- Wholesalers
- Fix & flip investors
- Public adjusters
- Attorneys

Per-vertical scoring weights determine which signals are surfaced
most prominently for each user.

## Coverage

Currently operating in Florida, Texas, and the broader Gulf Coast.
New counties are added as demand justifies. If a user asks about a
specific ZIP, direct them to forcedactionleads.com or to support.
